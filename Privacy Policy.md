# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: August 26, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how the TikTok Video Automation Tool (the "Application") handles data. The Application is a localized or private automation tool built to upload video content to your own TikTok account.

## 1. No Third-Party User Data Collection
We do not operate external databases, and we do not collect, harvest, store, or sell any personal data belonging to third-party users. The Application interacts directly and exclusively with your designated TikTok account.

## 2. Information Accessed and Processed
To fulfill its single purpose of uploading videos, the Application requests authorization to access specific permissions through the official TikTok API (such as the Video Kit or Content Posting API). This involves processing:
* **Authentication Credentials:** Secure OAuth access tokens required to authorize video uploads to your account.
* **Video Content:** The local video files, captions, and scheduling data that you explicitly choose to upload.

## 3. Local Storage and Data Retention
* All authentication tokens, API configurations, and video assets are stored locally on your own environment or runtime instance.
* No data is transmitted to any external third-party server, except for the secure payload sent directly to official TikTok API endpoints (`developers.tiktok.com`) to execute your upload requests.
* Data remains on your local storage until you delete the application configuration or clear your authorization cache.

## 4. Third-Party Services (TikTok)
Because this Application interacts with the TikTok platform, your usage is also governed by the TikTok Privacy Policy. We strongly encourage you to review the TikTok Privacy Policy to understand how TikTok processes your data once content is uploaded to their platform.

## 5. Data Security
We prioritize the security of your authentication tokens by utilizing standard API communication practices. However, because this tool runs in your own environment, you are responsible for securing the device or server hosting the application from unauthorized access.

## 6. Children's Privacy
The Application does not intentionally or knowingly target or collect information from children under the age of 13.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be indicated by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.

## 8. Contact Information
If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or data handling, please contact your support email or open an issue in the project repository.
